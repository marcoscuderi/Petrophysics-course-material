Uniaxial compression

Axial stress changes both length and diameter. Geometry controls specimen stiffness; the material modulus does not depend on specimen size.

εa = σa / E
εl = −ν εa
εv = εa + 2εl
k = EA0 / L0

Hydrostatic compression

Equal pressure acts in every direction. Shape is preserved while volume decreases.

K = E / [3(1 − 2ν)]
εv = p / K
ε1 = ε2 = ε3 = εv / 3

Simple shear

Shear changes shape. For small elastic strains, it does not change volume.

G = E / [2(1 + ν)]
γ = τ / G
εv ≈ 0

Try: vary ν across its full range. Compare the change in G with the much stronger change in K.
