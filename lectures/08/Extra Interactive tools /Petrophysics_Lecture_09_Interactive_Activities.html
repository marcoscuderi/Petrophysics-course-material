Connect evidence to physical process

Match each independent observation to the most direct interpretation it supports.

Microstructure excerpts from Aben et al. (2020), Fig. 10a-b, quasi-statically ruptured Lanhélin granite.

Use the most direct interpretation for each observation.
Loading closes compliant cracks. New cracks then open and reduce stiffness. Crack interaction concentrates deformation. The final microstructure preserves this localized damage.
How to run this in class
  1. Let students complete the matches individually.
  2. Ask which observation identifies timing and which identifies spatial localization.
  3. Reveal the synthesis only after discussing why no single measurement proves the entire interpretation.
